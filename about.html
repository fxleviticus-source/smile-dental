Who We Are

Dentistry Built Around Comfort

Smile Dental Clinic sits on Bwinjimfumu Road in Siliwiziya, Lusaka. We designed our practice around a simple idea: dental visits should feel calm, clear and unhurried, whether you're in for a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure.

Our care is organised into four straightforward categories — general, restorative, orthodontic & cosmetic, and advanced procedures — so you always know what to expect from your visit. We keep our doors open six days a week, plus Sunday mornings, to make appointments easier to fit around work and family life.
