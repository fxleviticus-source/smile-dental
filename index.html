About Smile Dental Clinic

Comfortable Care. Confident Smiles.

Smile Dental Clinic is located on Bwinjimfumu Road in Siliwiziya, Lusaka, offering dental care across four areas: general, restorative, orthodontic & cosmetic, and advanced procedures. We keep our hours flexible — open six days a week plus Sunday mornings — so quality dental care fits around your schedule.

  • Full oral examinations & digital X-rays
  • Restorative treatments — fillings, crowns & bridges
  • Orthodontics, clear aligners & teeth whitening
  • Root canal therapy & surgical extractions
